# Returns Policy

**Company:** Souq & Co (fictional)  
**Audience:** support  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective from:** 2025-01-01

Customers may return eligible items within **30 days of delivery**.

**Returnable categories:** Fitness Gear, Electronics. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

**Non-returnable categories:** Protein, Vitamins, Pre-Workout, Wellness. For health and safety reasons, opened or
unopened consumable products cannot be returned, **except** when the product arrived damaged,
expired, or was the wrong item. In those cases the customer must report it within 7 days of
delivery with a photo.

Return requests after 30 days are rejected unless a support lead approves an exception.
